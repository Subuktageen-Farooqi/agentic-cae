# Joints and fasteners

Choose a joint by the load path, required motion, service needs, available access, and manufacturing process. A removable bolted joint needs tool access and a credible way to resist shear and separation. A permanent joint may reduce part count but can make inspection and replacement harder.

# Bearings and linear guides

Bearings and guides support controlled motion; they do not remove the need to define load direction, alignment, contamination, lubrication, life, and mounting accuracy. Catalog selection depends on the actual supplier data and operating conditions. Do not infer capacity from appearance.

# Assembly and disassembly

Plan an assembly sequence and ask whether every fastener, tool, and replaceable item remains accessible. Use interfaces that locate parts consistently without unnecessary constraints. Preserve supplier and equipment context when a process or purchased component imposes a limitation.
